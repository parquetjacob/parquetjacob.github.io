**Question.** Which psychosocial determinants are associated with differential retention in health research. 

**Data and methods.** Study data was ascertained from the Midlife in the United States longitudinal health and well-being survey. Generalized estimating equations with family as the clustering unit were used to assess the association between psychosocial measures and retention status
