**Question.** Is the Perceived Stress Scale (PSS-10) reliable and valid for use in midlife adults, and does it align with biological stress markers?

**Methods.** Using MIDUS biomarker data in R, I evaluated internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha), compared one- and two-factor structures with confirmatory factor analysis, and tested convergent, discriminant, and criterion validity.

**Findings.** The scale showed strong internal consistency, and a two-factor (Helplessness / Self-Efficacy) structure fit better than a unidimensional model. Its limited alignment with C-reactive protein suggests the PSS is better suited to public health surveillance than to biomarker research.
