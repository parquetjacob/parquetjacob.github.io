**Question.** How do housing policies such as zoning and redlining shape racial health disparities?

**Methods.** I extended a NetLogo segregation model with an SEIR disease module and a composite health score driven by wealth, land value, and hospital quality, then tested 12+ policy scenarios across 200+ agents.

**Findings.** Redlining reproduced zoning-level segregation through less overt rules, and structural health penalties alone widened the majority/minority health gap independent of income.
