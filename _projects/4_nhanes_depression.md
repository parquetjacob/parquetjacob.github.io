**Question.** Is self-reported cognitive difficulty associated with depression among U.S. adults aged 50 and older?

**Methods.** Using nationally representative NHANES data (2021-2023, n = 4,683) in Stata, I ran bivariate tests and hierarchical logistic and linear regression, assessing confounding by diabetes, cholesterol, and medication use, and effect modification by general health status.

**Findings.** Difficulty remembering or concentrating was strongly associated with depression (adjusted OR = 3.63).
